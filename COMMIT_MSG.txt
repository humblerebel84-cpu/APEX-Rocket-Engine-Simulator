  + boundary layer); ideal model and EquationPanel stay uncorrected

Propellant DB Phase 2 (three liquid bipropellants, all CEA-validated):
- lf2_lh2   Tc 4150 K, M 0.0108, k 1.33, O/F 9.0 -> 488 s @ 68 bar, eps 77
- irfna_udmh Tc 3130 K, M 0.0210, k 1.23, O/F 3.3 -> 322 s @ 70 bar, eps 40
- h2o2_rp1  Tc 2960 K, M 0.0199, k 1.21, O/F 7.0 -> 327 s @ 70 bar, eps 40

Constants were calibrated against NASA CEA run locally (rocketcea 1.2.3),
not taken from recalled tables. Each model Isp lands within 0.2% of its CEA
equilibrium reference; model c* sits +3.1/+4.3/+4.8% vs CEA c*, in family
with the existing entries (-1.1% to +6.2%).

Each fills a structural gap: no storable *bipropellant* existed, no
nitric-acid oxidizer existed, and LOX/LH2 was silently teaching a false
performance ceiling. The +-3% CEA discipline now rests on 11 independent
reference points instead of 8.

Decouple approxModel from category. The old integrity test asserted
approxModel === !(Cryogenic || Hypergolic) — a proxy that breaks as soon as
a storable liquid biprop exists. Replaced with the real invariant: the set
of non-approx propellants must equal the CEA_VALIDATED list that lives
beside the regression cases, plus Solid/Hybrid/Monoprop are always approx.
Adding a non-approx propellant without a regression case now fails the build.

Also adds three ShowcaseCompare trade studies (The Chemical Ceiling,
Oxidizer Swap, Green vs Toxic Storables) and two like-for-like swap tests
pinning the teaching deltas (+35 s fluorine, -15 s nitric acid).

125 tests, lint, typecheck and build green.
